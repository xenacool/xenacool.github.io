Gallery

Larger applications built from bevy_composable_dragging and bevy_gradmesh. Each one downloads on its own when you press Run.

Kart racer editor

A track editor. Pull the control points of a closed loop to bend the road, pull the width handle to widen it, and drag the land up or down to raise hills under it.

Cozy castle

A castle builder. Choose pieces from a radial menu, stack them, open arches in walls with a gate, and stretch walls with a scale gizmo. Every drop is previewed while the pointer is still down.

Physics lab

A drag-and-drop editor for physics scenarios. Place gradmesh shapes on a grid, tune colliders, timestep and friction from a radial menu, then press Play and grab the bodies while avian simulates them.
